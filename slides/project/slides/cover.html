Who pays when nature strikes?

The insurance protection gap for natural catastrophes in Europe, 1980–2024

Group 9 · Amine Benbachir, Ylias Guetari, Marius Rang, Edouard Souied

BSc AIDAMS, ESSEC · 9 November 2026

Data: EIOPA protection-gap dashboard (2025)
and EEA annual losses (2026)

Welcome. We are Group 9. Our question: when a natural catastrophe hits Europe, who ends up paying for the damage? Two datasets, both built on the same CATDAT loss records: EIOPA’s protection-gap dashboard for the cross-section, and the EEA’s country-year series for the trend.
