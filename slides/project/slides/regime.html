The figure is now flood only, so the regime colour and the gap refer to the same peril. Countries that lose more to floods are not better insured: Slovenia is the most flood-intensive and 96% uninsured. The public-involvement group is mixed: Iceland, Denmark, France, Norway low; Spain and Belgium near Germany. On the right, the apparent contradiction about state relief: systematic relief looked good, 47% against 89%. Split by scheme, it is 29% inside public schemes and 93% outside them, the same as ad-hoc relief. So relief does not raise cover by itself; outside a scheme it is consistent with charity hazard.
